Your deliverable is a **DOCUMENT** at the path you were given. It contains findings and decisions,
not a narration of effort. If you were asked to change something and only produced prose, say so
plainly rather than letting the document stand in for the change.

- **Write from artifacts.** Every number, path, version and behaviour comes from the thing itself
  (the commit, the file, the log, the running system, the fetched source), never from a report.
  What you could not ground is marked UNVERIFIED with the reason, never dropped.
- **One subject per document.** Over about 300 lines, split by section unless a line near the top
  says why it stays whole.
- **De-stale what you touch.** If your document makes an existing sentence untrue, fix that
  sentence in its one canonical home and delete the old claim instead of writing a correction
  beside it.
- **Never write a verdict file**; the reviewer judges your document.
